Loremaster is a campaign memory tool for tabletop RPG groups. It turns a session recording or notes into a cinematic recap, tracks the characters and places your party has met, and lets anyone at the table ask, "wait, what happened with that NPC?" and actually get an answer.

Though blood was spilled and Migli nearly fell, they stood their ground, learning quickly that the path ahead demands more than just shared gold.

A Word
About AI

AI is a sensitive topic in tabletop spaces, so we want to be plain. Loremaster does not generate campaigns, replace GMs, or replace player creativity. The story is still yours. The tool handles bookkeeping, recaps, and recall so the people at the table can spend their time playing.
