Loremaster is a campaign memory tool for tabletop RPG groups. It turns a session recording or notes into a cinematic recap, tracks the people, places, and events in your story, and lets anyone at the table ask, "wait, what happened with that NPC?" and get an answer pulled from what the party actually said and did.

Though blood was spilled and Migli nearly fell, they stood their ground, learning quickly that the path ahead demands more than just shared gold.

What it does
  1. I.
    Session recaps

    An automatically generated cinematic summary, ready before the next session.

  2. II.
    Campaign memory

    Characters, places, artifacts, and events captured as the party experiences them.

  3. III.
    Searchable history

    Ask the campaign anything and receive a narrated response, grounded in real play.

A Word
About AI

AI is a sensitive topic in tabletop spaces, so we want to be plain. Loremaster does not generate campaigns, replace GMs, or replace player creativity. The story is still yours. The tool handles bookkeeping, recaps, and recall so the people at the table can spend their time playing.
